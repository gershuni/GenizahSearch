Terms of Use — Dicta Genizah Search

Effective date: 10.10.26

Who we are

Dicta Genizah Search ("GenizahSearch," "the site," "we") is a free research platform for Cairo Genizah manuscripts at genizahsearch.com. It includes a web app, a Windows desktop app, a public Search API, and AI integrations — an MCP server at https://genizahsearch.com/mcp that ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini users can connect to, and (for as long as it lasts) the GenizahSearch custom GPT in ChatGPT.

GenizahSearch is published by Hillel Gershuni, with the support of Dicta — The Israel Center for Text Analysis.

1. AI answers can be wrong

ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini (through the MCP server) and the GPT can search the corpus and describe what they find, but they can also misread, mistranslate, or misattribute a manuscript, or simply get something wrong. These tools are a research aid, not a source of truth. Before relying on or publishing a claim an AI tool gave you, check it against the actual manuscript images, the transcriptions, and the catalogue records on the site.

GenizahSearch brings together work from many sources, and each one keeps its own rights:

  • FJMS and the National Library of Israel — catalogue descriptions and bibliography.
