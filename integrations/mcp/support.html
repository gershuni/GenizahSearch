Reporting a wrong or failed AI answer

If ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI assistant using GenizahSearch gave a wrong answer or an error, please send:

  • which assistant you used, and roughly when;
  • what you asked, and what it answered or the error it showed (a screenshot helps);
  • the shelfmark or page link involved, if there was one.

Please do not post private job identifiers publicly. An AI answer is a research aid: check claims against the manuscript, its transcription and its catalogue record (see the terms of use).
