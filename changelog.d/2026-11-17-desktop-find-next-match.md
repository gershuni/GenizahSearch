## [Unreleased] -- Find in text goes from match to match (desktop, branch `find-next-match`)

### Improvements

- **Find in text moves between its matches** (Browse by Shelfmark, View All included, and the
  Manuscript Viewer): a count beside the box shows which match you are on ("3/12"), marked in
  orange while the others stay yellow. Enter or the down arrow goes to the next match, Shift+Enter
  or the up arrow to the previous one (F3 / Shift+F3 too), round from the last to the first; a
  match off screen is scrolled into view. Typing starts from the first match on screen. Asked for
  by a reader, for long manuscripts where the matches are pages apart. The keyboard shortcuts card
  and Help name the keys.
