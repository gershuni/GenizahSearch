<objective>
Establish the identity lifecycle and session foundation for Phase 114: add the `ACTIVE_PING` enum member, build the startup identity coordinator that SPLITS always-on identity reconciliation from the one-shot `session_start` (consent → identity-sync → session_start), expose the `_telemetry_ready()` producer gate, wire `identify()`/`reset_identity()` at login/logout/opt-in, and emit best-effort `desktop_session_end` on clean exit. This plan also creates the Wave-0 test scaffold (`tests/test_telemetry_phase114.py`) that downstream plans extend.

Purpose: All other Phase 114 producers reference `self._session_id`, gate on `self._telemetry_ready()`, and depend on identity being resolved first. This is the ordering-critical foundation (D-10, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-14, D-15). Getting `_uuid` (not `.id`) right is what makes the cross-surface web↔desktop journey real (the headline value of this milestone). Splitting identity-sync from session_start (REVIEWS HIGH-4) is what keeps a mid-session opt-out→opt-in identified; the `_telemetry_ready()` gate (REVIEWS MEDIUM-9) closes the on_startup_finished→coordinator race.

Output: `ACTIVE_PING` in `DesktopEvent`; `_run_startup_telemetry_coordinator`, `_sync_telemetry_identity`, `_telemetry_ready`, `_setup_active_ping` stub call site, session_end, and login/logout identity wiring in `genizah_app.py`; `tests/test_telemetry_phase114.py` with identity/session/re-opt-in tests.
</objective>
