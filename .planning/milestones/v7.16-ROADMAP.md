## Overview

A focused desktop-only milestone that made LOCAL ("My Library") Hebrew PDF search actually work. The dominant text-layer failure mode — justified/emphasised Hebrew typesetting shattering words into single letters (e.g. אוצר הגאונים indexed ~74% one-letter "words", so `פירוש המשנה` became `פירו ש ה מ ש נה` and was unsearchable), while tightly-set books fused whole phrases into one token — was fixed by rewriting `extract_pdf_pages` onto a per-glyph `rawdict` foundation with per-line adaptive word-boundary detection and RTL-gated reconstruction. Bundled new file-management actions for LOCAL hits and three significant search/startup performance fixes surfaced during real-library UAT.

Validated against the real corpus by Spike 001 (`.planning/spikes/001-meiri-glyph-reorder-vs-current/`).

## Milestone Summary

**Decimal / inserted phases:** none (single formal phase 102; remaining work no-phase).

**Key Decisions:**

- rawdict-primary extraction (per-glyph bbox) over PyMuPDF `get_text` line strings — the only foundation that lets word boundaries be re-derived from actual glyph spacing. (Good)
- RTL-gated reorder — Meiri's reorder helps Hebrew order/headers/brackets but HURTS Latin (Spike 001), so it is gated to RTL/Hebrew content; LTR stays on the non-reordered path. No LTR regression. (Good)
- Per-line 1-D Otsu word-gap valley over a global fixed fraction — intra/inter-word gaps overlap across book classes but are bimodal *per line*. (Good — replaced the first-cut fixed-floor/median that shattered/merged)
- Unicode-`Mn` combining-mark classification over a fixed `0x05B0–0x05C7` range — the range mis-treated maqaf/sof-pasuq as vowels and missed te'amim. (Good)
- No auto-flip of `extraction_format_version` on launch — bulk re-extraction must stay on the background worker; auto-flip froze a 12K-PDF library (Phase 101 D-04 lesson). Recovery via the manual "Re-index All" button. (Good)
- Search history stores no result snapshots; clicking a history entry re-runs the search — the alternative (storing `results[:5000]`) grew `search_history.json` to 778 MB. (Good)
- LOCAL freeze fixes diagnosed by measuring on the real quarantined SQLite/data, not guessing; Claude + Codex investigated in parallel and converged. (Good)

**Issues Resolved:** D-F13, D-F13b, D-F13c, D-F13d, D-F14 (reframed), D-F16 (corrupt-encoding flag), D-F19, D-F20, D-F21, D-F22 (BLOCKER), D-F23 (three freezes, BLOCKER), D-F24, D-F25.
