<objective>
Make works findable by neutral title, and fold computed identifications into the existing catalogue
browse page — visibly separated from catalogued ones and never presented under shared wording.

Purpose: the catalogue page's work vocabulary is the catalogue's own, and it resolves shelfmarks
only after fetching a page, so the mapping between computed identifications and catalogued works is
inherently incomplete. The obligation here is honesty, not completeness: the two vocabularies must
never be rendered under a shared "identified as" wording that would imply the catalogue asserted
what we computed, or vice versa.
Output: a shared title normalizer, the catalogue integration, and an honesty render-smoke.
</objective>
