<objective>
Render the panel body: two even panes, identification rows with their relation chips and coverage
labels, the manuscript pane naming its works, the related-pages section, the disclosure toggles and
the placeholder voting controls.

Purpose: the primary reader job on a manuscript page is understanding the whole manuscript, not
judging one row — manuscript-level coherence is what makes a single claim judgeable. One sampled
page's identification looks arbitrary alone and obviously right once you see that the surrounding
folios carry the same author's commentaries in the standard order. That is why the manuscript
picture gets equal weight and why it must name the works.
Output: the panel body, rendered entirely from the prebuilt display model.
</objective>
