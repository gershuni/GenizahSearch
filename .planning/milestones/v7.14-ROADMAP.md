## Goal

Productize Yehuda Seewald's external prototype (`seewald_addition/`) as a first-class in-app desktop feature: point GenizahSearch at folders of `.docx`/`.pdf`/`.txt` (later `.html`/`.xlsx`/`.csv`) documents and have them indexed into a separate Tantivy side-index merged into normal Search / Composition / Parallels results with a clear `LOCAL` badge and a corpus selector — while keeping personal corpora entirely off the cloud. Then scale the feature to the prototype's real working size (13K files / 43 GB), harden crash recovery, and ensure no NLI/IIIF upstream slowdown can hang the web app.
