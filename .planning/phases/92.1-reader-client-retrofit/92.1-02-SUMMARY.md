## Decisions Made

- **Checkpoint-pause at Task 1** rather than guessing a case branch and applying a fix without diagnostic evidence. The plan explicitly forbids "freezing `auth_session` at dialog-open and replaying later" (rejected per Reviews H-AGREED-1 — both Gemini and Codex independently flagged this as a stale-token footgun), so picking a case without evidence would risk applying a `copy_context()` fix in case-a (where it's a silent no-op) or a retry-on-AssertionError fallback in case-b/c (where the real fix is contextvar binding, not retry logic). Hillel-driven reproduction is the only way to discriminate.
- **Diagnostic local to `add_to_list_dialog.py`** (Revision Warning 7) — not in `safe_storage.py` — preserves Phase 87 lint allowlist (empty) and Phase 91 atomic-write test invariants.
- **All 4 capture points (P1+P2+P3+P3.5)** rather than just P3 — per Reviews R2-2 Codex round-2 HIGH catch, single-point P3-only capture is insufficient because the case-(b) vs case-(a) discrimination requires knowing whether the contextvar was bound at dialog ENTRY time, not just at handler firing time. Single-P3 capture would silently endorse `copy_context()` even when it cannot help.
