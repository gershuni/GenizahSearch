## Authoritative asset-state model (HIGH-1 / HIGH-2 / MEDIUM-6)
- `web/atlas_assets.py` owns ALL asset state. `ATLAS_DATA_DIR` = repo-root `atlas_data/` (computed from `os.path.dirname(os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(__file__)))`), OUTSIDE `web/static/`.

- Deploy posture (HIGH-2): the asset is scp'd asset-first then the process is restarted (plan 133-06), so the startup load is authoritative; no per-request `os.path.exists` and no mixing of startup-load with per-request stat.
