<objective>
Build the corpus-wide findings query: three selectable row units, the facet cascade on the identified
work, the filter and sort set, and a total that can actually be displayed.

Purpose: the previous shape measured 3.41–3.55 seconds against a 1.5 second cap because it scanned
all display claims and built a temporary sort structure, and the deduped identification count alone
measured 16 seconds. The materialized identification grain exists precisely so this query can meet
its budget. And the facet axis is a correctness question, not a performance one: one manuscript
catalogued as court records carries a verifiably correct commentary finding, and 338 top-band
findings sit on manuscripts catalogued documentary or legal.
Output: the query layer, its tests, and the benchmark gate.
</objective>
