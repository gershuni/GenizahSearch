<objective>
Ship `/work/{id}`: every identified carrier of a work, grouped by codicological witness unit, with
AND-composed filters, a three-way sort, server-side paging and a visible real total.

Purpose: this is the surface that turns a single identification into a corpus view — the reader can
ask "what else carries this work" and get an answer grouped the way a codicologist would group it.
Joined fragments are one witness, not several, which is why the totals count units rather than claim
rows.
Output: the route, the page, and its render-smoke suite.
</objective>
