<objective>
Give the findings page its domain facet: a one-time curated assignment of an FJMS domain to each work
carrying a shipped claim, plus the small author-alias fill — both as hash-pinned artifacts, not as
hand edits to a database.

Purpose: `works.genre` is entirely NULL today, so the domain facet is inert without this. The work
facet needs nothing (the works being identified ARE the discovery works, 100% covered) and bridging
discovery titles to the catalogue's own title vocabulary matches only 5% and is not required — so this
curation pass is the only new data the whole facet cascade needs. Feasibility is measured: 90 of 93
sampled works were high-confidence in a single pass with no external lookups, extrapolating to roughly
3-4% needing a lookup or an owner ruling.
Output: two pinned artifacts, a validator, and a short list of rows the owner must rule on.
</objective>
