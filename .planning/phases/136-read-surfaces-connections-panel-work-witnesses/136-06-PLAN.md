<objective>
Persist the two sort/filter keys the new surfaces need and cannot compute at request time:
direct-family matched-letter coverage, and the materialized band rank — plus the indexes a
corpus-wide findings query requires.

Purpose: coverage is already computed at every direct-witness ingestion for the Lever-1 routing
decision and then thrown away, so `coverage_ppm` and `band_rank` do not exist as columns and the
coverage filter is inert. A representative findings ordering measured 3.41–3.55 s against a 1.5 s
cap because it scanned all display claims and built a temporary B-tree.
Output: two new indexed evidence columns and the D-10a index set.
</objective>
