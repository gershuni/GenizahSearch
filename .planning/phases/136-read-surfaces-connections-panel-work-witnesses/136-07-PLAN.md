<objective>
Ship the main-pool rule and the display-grouping predicates as pure, fully tested modules — before any
build script or render function touches them.

Purpose: the buckets on both surfaces are drawn by four non-compensating gates over the identification
(manuscript × canonical work), not by a score and not by a band-derived confidence scale. The
cautionary example is concrete: sketch 003's hand-picked `confOf()` disagreed with the predicate the
codebase already had and rendered the best-measured population in the system as "Weak". One rule, one
implementation, one wording.
Output: two pure modules the bake, the panel and the findings page all call.
</objective>
