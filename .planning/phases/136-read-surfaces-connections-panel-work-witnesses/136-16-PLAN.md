<objective>
Ship the corpus-wide findings page's shell: its route, its availability-gated nav entry, the reserved
launch-headline slot, the permanent caveat slot, the mode strip, the filter bar with its facet cascade
and its "more matches" control, the result bar and the pager.

Purpose: this is the surface the owner described as the reason the milestone is worth shipping —
"a big new amazing feature… maximum ability to see new findings" — and it is also where the caveat has
to be given a designed home rather than buried. Building the shell separately from the rows lets the
panel track and this track proceed in parallel, and it keeps the honesty-critical chrome (the caveat,
the disabled-filter treatment, the mode tags, the second bucket's reachability) reviewable on its own.
Output: a routable, flag-gated page with every control present and correctly disabled.
</objective>

**Revised 2026-08-04 after Codex pre-flight round 5 (findings 1, 2, 9, 10).** Three owner rulings (R, T,
U) were recorded on 2026-08-03, AFTER this plan was written and after the round-4 sign-off, so no part of
it could reference them; and plan 136-14 has since built the service layer this page reads.

- **Finding 1 (BLOCKER).** Ruling U — the launch leads with the contribution figure and its three
  shades — had no implementation owner. The page header here was specified only as title, sub-line and
  caveat. **Split:** this plan reserves the headline REGION as a named, structurally-present slot and

  reader; plan **136-18** (wave 9) fills the slot. This plan is wave 7 and therefore CANNOT consume

- **Finding 2 (HIGH), ruling T.** The "more matches" bucket holds roughly half the non-Bible discovery
  value in the release. This plan mentioned a toggle but no acceptance criterion asserted that the
  control exists, is prominent, works, is match-framed, or is unnumbered — so a page omitting it
  entirely satisfied every criterion. Criteria that would FAIL are added below.
- **Finding 9 (HIGH), ruling R.** Work-facet labels arrive as RAW titles from the service
  (`get_findings_facets_enveloped` selects `MIN(w.neutral_title)`), so the facet list must route them
  through `display_work_title` too.
- **Finding 10 (MEDIUM).** The off-loop instruction pointed at `catalog_browse`'s `run.io_bound` model,
  which is the WRONG model here: `web/discovery.py`'s wrappers are already async and already dispatch to
  an executor internally, so a `run.io_bound` around them is a nested offload burning two threadpool
  slots per request on a single-worker server. `tests/test_no_await_sync_function.py` cannot see any of
  that — it only detects `await` on a LOCALLY defined sync `def`.

**Revised again 2026-08-04 after Codex pre-flight round 6 (finding 6).** The round-5 criteria asserted
the "more matches" control's `display:none` state and its DOM ancestry, and that an interaction changed
a service call. None of that is the same claim as "a reader can click it": a directly-invoked handler
passes an interaction assertion with an inert DOM binding, and a child can be `display:block` under an
ancestor collapsed by visibility, height, clipping or a framework expansion container, which an ancestry
check cannot see. Ruling T makes this the reachability of ~2,189 non-Bible candidate identifications —
roughly half the non-Bible value in the release — so the criterion is now operability in a real browser,
with its own positive control, and its absence is NOT MET rather than a skip.

The headline criterion also stopped naming specific launch figures: fixing a list of digits into a test
rots the moment the artifact moves, which is the same defect ruling U was issued to prevent. This plan
asserts the region is digit-FREE; the figure-specific guard is 136-22's, and its glob-derived scope
already covers `web/pages/findings.py`.

**Revised a third time 2026-08-04 after Codex pre-flight round 7 (finding 7).** Round 6 scoped 136-17's
dispatch criterion per service state and left this plan's own unscoped: it required exactly one internal
executor dispatch "per findings request". The live wrapper short-circuits with ZERO dispatches when
`discovery_available()` is False (`web/discovery.py` ≈229), and the route has its own unavailable early
return, while the page must still render the outage. As written, a correct outage implementation fails
the criterion. It is now scoped to an available, cache-cold, successful request, with zero on the
unavailable route required explicitly rather than left to be read as a defect.
