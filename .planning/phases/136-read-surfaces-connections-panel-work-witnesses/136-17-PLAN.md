    something, that decision belongs in the model — that is the whole point of the split, and it is what
    makes the honesty invariants testable without a browser.

    Layout: the panel header with its counted entry chip, the permanent caveat slot between header and
    body, then the two panes — "On this page" first, "Elsewhere in this manuscript" second — as a block
    stack on mobile and a `1fr 1fr` grid at 900px and above. Both panes carry equal weight; the
    manuscript pane is NOT collapsed. On mobile they stack page-first, with nothing hidden.

    Rows in the documented order: verb plus work title (plain TEXT, not a link — `/work/{id}` does not
    exist until Phase 136.1, and a dead link is worse than plain text), then the meta line beginning
    with the relation chip carrying the band label as its `title` tooltip, then the optional granularity
    sub-line, then the optional low-coverage note, then the actions with the placeholder vote controls
    pushed to the inline end.

    Keep the chip visually neutral. Colour-coding it by relation kind reintroduces per-tier confidence
    styling through the back door, which is exactly what the requirement prohibits.

    Manuscript pane: NAMED works with their page counts, chips solid-and-tinted for works on this folio
    and dashed-and-dimmed for works reachable only behind the screening gate, paging above the
    threshold. A bare count is not acceptable here — manuscript-level coherence is what makes a single
    claim judgeable.

    Other manuscripts matching this work: an on-demand expansion per row, driven by the model's
    descriptor and fetched lazily through the enveloped wrapper when the reader opens it — never loaded
    with the panel. It lists the other carriers with their library and shelfmark, displays the WEAKER
    band of the pair, and paginates with a real total. Its heading uses the match-framing string
    ("Other manuscripts matching ⟨work⟩"), never a phrase asserting identity.

    Related pages: header and count by default, labelled as unevaluated candidate alignments, rows only
    behind the toggle.

    **The catalogue description must never sit unlabelled beside a page-level claim (D-13i).** Browse's
    compact metadata header carries the shelfmark's catalogue line, and on a composite shelfmark that
    line describes OTHER leaves. The second mockup pass produced a false alarm exactly this way: a
    manuscript catalogued as court records carried a verifiably correct commentary identification on one
    folio, and the two read side by side as an absurd mismatch when the claim was right and the
    catalogue was coarse. Either omit the catalogue description from the panel's vicinity or label it
    explicitly as describing the SHELFMARK rather than this folio. Implement one of the two and say
    which in the summary.

    Disclosure toggles: exactly the levels the model emits — no more, no fewer.

    Placeholder voting controls render inert and are wired in a later phase; they must not imply that a
    vote was recorded.

    Bilingual and RTL from line one, phone-first.
  </action>
