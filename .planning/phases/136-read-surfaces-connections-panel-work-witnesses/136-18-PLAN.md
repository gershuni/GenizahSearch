to overclaim. The line deliberately not crossed is "new discovery": that stacks two unearned claims —
that the match is correct, and that it is new — on a row carrying no human review until a later phase.
A wrong match that no catalogue records is not a discovery. The shipped wording asserts candidacy, and
only candidacy.
