<objective>
Build the committed, masked offline distillation (`scripts/build_discovery_sidecar.py` + the `scripts/discovery_build/` submodules) that turns the gitignored research DB into a `discovery.db` carrying opaque work_ids, masked `source_corpus`, both claim families, one band per claim key, witness units, and evidence stored ONLY as offsets + text_layer + snapshot hash — and that emits the neutral-title review artifact (source masked) for the owner gate in 134-07.

Purpose: The correctness+masking heart of the phase. It forks the DB-READ SHAPE of the gitignored probe pipeline but re-mints opaque ids and structurally cannot ship raw research ids, raw `cat`, or unreviewed M-source titles. Pure transformation logic is unit-tested so CI proves masking discipline without the 3.1 GB DB; the full end-to-end run + owner review happen in 134-07. Because this is the densest, highest-risk correctness+masking work, the claims-family logic and the witness-unit logic are split into separate tasks.
Output: `scripts/build_discovery_sidecar.py`, `scripts/discovery_build/{works,claims,units,contract}.py`, `tests/test_discovery_build.py`.
</objective>
