<objective>
Build the committed, masked offline distillation (`scripts/build_discovery_sidecar.py` + the `scripts/discovery_build/` submodules) that turns the gitignored research DB into a `discovery.db` carrying opaque work_ids, masked `source_corpus` (on works AND both claim tables), both claim families (built with EXACT per-source queries), one band per claim key, witness units, and evidence stored ONLY as offsets + text_layer + per-side snapshot hash — and that emits the neutral-title review artifact (a CANDIDATE CSV schema, source masked) and consumes an owner-approved file through a fresh validated `--from-approved` importer (a DISTINCT APPROVED CSV schema) for the owner gate in 134-07.

Purpose: The correctness+masking heart of the phase. It forks the DB-READ SHAPE of the gitignored probe pipeline but re-mints opaque ids (anchored to a durable crosswalk), masks source via a build-injected mapper, and structurally cannot ship raw research ids, raw `cat`, or unreviewed M-source titles. Pure transformation logic is unit-tested so CI proves masking discipline without the 3.1 GB DB; the full end-to-end run + owner review happen in 134-07. Because this is the densest, highest-risk correctness+masking work, the works, claims-family, and witness-unit logic are split into separate tasks.
Output: `scripts/build_discovery_sidecar.py`, `scripts/discovery_build/{works,claims,units,contract,review_artifact}.py`, `tests/test_discovery_build.py`.
</objective>
