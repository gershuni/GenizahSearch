<objective>
Add the fail-closed, manifest-resolved versioned startup loader for `discovery.db` (`web/discovery_assets.py`), the `DISCOVERY_ENABLED` feature flag (distinct from the atlas-preview flag), and the `web/main.py` startup wiring — modeled 1:1 on the Phase-133-hardened `web/atlas_assets.py` (including its `manifest.json` + content-hash resolution model).

Purpose: This is the DATA-07/DATA-08 fail-open + reject-incompatible mechanism. Resolving via a `manifest.json` (the ONE canonical `discovery-v1-<hash>.db` + a `content_hash` the loader validates) — never a bare filename glob — mirrors atlas and removes the ambiguity of multiple hashed files after a rollback. Loading + validating once at startup (never per-request) and gating every surface on the single `discovery_available()` predicate means a flag-OFF or absent/corrupt/incompatible sidecar hides cleanly with zero errors while the rest of the app stays fully up.
Output: `web/discovery_assets.py`, `web/feature_flags.py` (extended), `web/main.py` (startup call), `tests/test_discovery_loader.py`, `tests/test_discovery_flag.py`.
</objective>
