**Round 3 Codex HIGH #4 — variant loop preservation (REQUIRED, not optional).**
Earlier drafts of this plan offered two options for migrating the
`genizah_core.py:~3973-3987` Mosseri block: (a) delete the entire `for variant in variants:` loop, or
(b) keep the loop and call `get_cambridge_manifest_with_bridge(variant)` per variant. Option (a) is REJECTED.

Why: the live runtime iterates `call_numbers_raw` for the sys_id and breaks on first
match. The wrapper takes a single shelfmark — it has no way to enumerate the row's
variants on its own. If the primary `shelfmark` is not the constructible form but a
later variant in `call_numbers_raw` IS, deleting the loop loses that coverage.
Concretely: any Mosseri sys_id whose primary shelfmark is e.g. `Moss IIIA 27` but
whose alternative variant `Moss. III,27O` is the one `construct_mosseri_cudl_label`
would handle — that row would silently lose its CUDL manifest after option (a).

Mandate option (b) only: keep the loop, swap the inner call.
